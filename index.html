Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers before you begin

Still have questions? Call or text us and we will walk you through the process.

Is there any obligation to accept an offer?

No. You can review the offer and decide whether it works for you.

Do I need to pay an agent commission?

No. Discover Land LLC works directly with property owners, so there is no real-estate agent commission.

What information do you need about my land?

The APN, county, state, acreage, or a copy of a tax notice is usually enough to begin.

What types of land do you consider?

We review many types of vacant land, including inherited, remote, unused, and difficult-to-manage parcels.

How long does the process take?

The timing depends on the property and title work. We explain the expected timeline before you agree to proceed.

Ready to discuss your property?

Call, text, or email us. Include the property APN or county when possible.
