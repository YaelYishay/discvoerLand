Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers before you begin

Have a question about buying land? Call or email us to discuss where you are in your search.

What does a land buying consultancy do?

We help you clarify your goals, explore property information, and understand the questions to investigate before purchasing land.

How much does a consultation cost?

Contact us to discuss your needs. We will explain the proposed scope and any fees before you agree to a service.

What should I share to get started?

Share your preferred area, budget, intended use, and approximate acreage. If you have a property in mind, include its listing link or APN, county, and state.

Can you help if I have already found a property?

Yes. Share the parcel details and your questions so we can discuss the research and guidance that would be useful to you.

Does your guidance replace professional inspections or advice?

Our consultancy helps you organize information and plan your research. Legal, tax, title, survey, and engineering questions should be verified with qualified professionals, and permitted uses confirmed with the relevant local authorities.
